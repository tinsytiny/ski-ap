# Community Guidelines

**Do:** share what you saw on the mountain, be kind, be useful.
**Don't (blocked or held automatically):** profanity, threats or wishing harm, personal contact details, links, asking people to contact you elsewhere, repeated characters, shouting in capitals, safety claims such as "100% safe", duplicates. Mild insults are held for review.

**Posting rules:** sign in; be within 5 km of the mountain; slow down (limited posts per minute).
**Report and block:** every comment has Report and Block. Several reports hide a comment.
**Enforcement:** repeated problems lead to a 1 hour mute, then suspension. Content removed after it got through means a 24 hour chat ban; a repeat means chat is off until review. New accounts may be reviewed.
